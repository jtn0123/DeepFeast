# Expanded fish anatomy

Ten additions have separate body proportions and fin outlines in `ExpandedFishAnatomy.cs`. These are authored game models informed by the references below; they are not scans or exact biological reconstructions. Identity, scientific names and source URLs remain in the species catalog and review views, with no floating species label in normal play.

| Catalog key | Identity and reference | Geometry cues | Skin cues handled by the shared shader |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `almaco_jack` | [Almaco jack](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/almaco-jack), *Seriola rivoliana* | High-backed, compressed body; small first dorsal and much taller second dorsal; long anal base; forked tail. | Dusky olive/amber flank; dark diagonal mask through the eye; light belly. |
| `goliath_grouper` | [Atlantic goliath grouper](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-goliath-grouper), *Epinephelus itajara* | Broad head and shoulder; thick body; continuous notched dorsal with taller soft rear portion; rounded anal and tail outlines. | Olive/brown mottling and smaller dark spots. |
| `atlantic_halibut` | [Atlantic halibut](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-halibut), *Hippoglossus hippoglossus* | Very thin body through depth; broad eyed flank; continuous dorsal/anal margins; shallow concave tail. Both eyes belong on the same flank. | Brown mottled eyed side and pale blind side; arched lateral line. |
| `atlantic_mackerel` | [Atlantic mackerel](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-mackerel), *Scomber scombrus* | Slim spindle body; separated first/second dorsals; compact anal; five small finlets per margin; forked tail. | Blue-green back, silver belly and wavy upper-body bars. |
| `mahi_mahi` | [Atlantic mahi-mahi](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-mahi-mahi), *Coryphaena hippurus* | Adult male interpretation with steep forehead and long continuous dorsal banner; compressed body and long rear anal fin; narrow tail stalk. | Green/blue upper body, gold flanks, blue spots and contrasting dorsal. |
| `skipjack_tuna` | [Atlantic skipjack tuna](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-skipjack-tuna), *Katsuwonus pelamis* | Compact torpedo; narrow tail stalk; two separate dorsal fins; compact second dorsal/anal; seven small finlets per margin. | Purple-blue back, silver lower flank and conspicuous horizontal belly stripes. |
| `striped_bass` | [Atlantic striped bass](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-striped-bass), *Morone saxatilis* | Stout elongated body; separate notched spinous first dorsal and rounded second dorsal; compact rear anal. | Olive/silver body and continuous dark horizontal stripes. |
| `yellowfin_tuna` | [Atlantic yellowfin tuna](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/atlantic-yellowfin-tuna), *Thunnus albacares* | Slender torpedo; first dorsal plus long curved sickle second dorsal/anal; narrow peduncle; seven finlets per margin. | Metallic blue back, yellow lateral accent, bright yellow fins and finlets. |
| `bluefish` | [Bluefish](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/bluefish), *Pomatomus saltatrix* | Lean body; small first dorsal and long second dorsal; long anal base; pointed head. | Blue-green back and silver flank/belly. |
| `red_drum` | [Red drum](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/profiles/saltwater/drums/red-drum/), *Sciaenops ocellatus* | Arched elongated body; blunt front; separate spiny first and long soft second dorsal; short anal; shallow concave tail. | Copper body, pale belly and black ocellus at the tail stalk. |

Supplemental anatomy references: [Florida Museum goliath grouper](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/goliath-grouper/) describes its continuous notched dorsal, rounded pectorals and rounded caudal; [Florida Museum red drum](https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/discover-fish/species-profiles/red-drum/) documents two dorsal fins, an arched body and slightly concave caudal. The NOAA illustrations linked from the species pages guide the overall proportions. Colors are game-readable interpretations of those source descriptions rather than measured pigment values.

The margin fins, finlets and sickles share the existing combined fin mesh. Their roots follow the same `Profile` and `CenterY` as the body; the rim supplies the authored extension and curvature. Every fractional power of a sine uses a clamped nonnegative base. These additions keep the existing seven renderers, shared material and per-species mesh cache.

Integration uses `ExpandedBody` before generic anatomy, `BuildExpandedCaudal` before generic caudal, `BuildExpandedSpines` before generic dorsal/anal fins, and `AddExpandedFinDetails` after the main fins. The main anatomy builder owns eyes, pectorals and mouth treatment; the shader owns markings and colors. Native build, motion validation, reviewed captures and final tuning are recorded in the v9 review package by the integrating agent.
